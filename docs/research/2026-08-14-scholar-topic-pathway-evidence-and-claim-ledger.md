## Evidence model and decision meanings

- `source_backed_fact`: exact identity or bibliographic wording directly reproduced by the named source and locator.
- `editorial_synthesis`: Syntag wording that connects sources or defines a navigation taxonomy; it is not promoted to L1 because a source is cited nearby.
- `research_guidance`: theory selection, analysis-unit, materials, limitation, and risk guidance; it requires methods-aware human review.
- Every page row has `review_decision: pending_review`, `reviewer_identity: not_assigned`, `reviewer_role: not_assigned`, and `reviewed_at: not_assigned`.
- `deepen`: retain the bounded current entry and prepare additional claim rows only from reproducible source locators; it does not authorize prose expansion now.
- `narrow`: retain identity/navigation value but remove or rewrite an over-broad attribution, field boundary, or theory-role statement before implementation.
- `retain_bounded_entry`: keep the current short navigation job and safe blank fields; do not expand until evidence and review gates are complete.
- `hide`: propose a separate U3 visibility decision only when safe bounded public value cannot be retained. No page in this pack meets that threshold.
