## 5. Review of the three theory paths

### 5.1 `teacher-professional-development-theory` — `primary`

- **Object actually explained:** the connection between a specified professional-learning process and a specified change in teacher practice, including mediating processes and conditions.
- **Analysis unit:** teacher-learning processes and bounded practice-change episodes; the whole programme or school is not automatically the unit.
- **Materials needed:** evidence capable of speaking to the proposed process and change, such as accounts tied to concrete episodes, practice or artefact evidence, programme documents, contextual conditions, and countercases where feasible and ethical.
- **Insufficient materials:** attendance, satisfaction, exposure, a programme label, or a single untethered self-report.
- **Nature of the route:** the `primary` selection is L2 editorial synthesis. Suggested materials are L3 research guidance. Source descriptions of the named model are L1 candidates pending human review.
- **Participation-to-change risk:** present and explicitly material. Neither attendance nor participation establishes changed practice, learner outcomes, or causation.
- **CoP relabelling risk:** indirect but present if a professional-learning programme or PLC is treated as a CoP without its own practice evidence.
- **Review judgment required:** whether “primary/high” is the right editorial route and whether the Topic should name Clarke and Hollingsworth's Interconnected Model more explicitly instead of relying on the broader umbrella label.

### 5.2 `communities-of-practice` — `supporting`

- **Object actually explained:** learning through participation in a socially sustained shared practice, including how the Chapter 2 practice dimensions, access, and boundaries are organised. The complete names of those three dimensions remain pending lawful original-text review.
- **Analysis unit:** a shared practice and participation relations, not a formally named team, school, PLC, department, project, network, platform, or programme by default.
- **Materials needed:** repeated practice episodes, interaction/participation evidence, locally used artefacts, negotiated work and accountability, access or boundary cases, and participant accounts interpreted in context.
- **Insufficient materials:** a meeting calendar, membership list, formal objective, shared handbook, programme name, co-location, or satisfaction survey.
- **Nature of the route:** `supporting` is L2 editorial synthesis. Candidate materials are L3 research guidance. LPP and Wenger's bounded social-participation vocabulary are L1 candidates pending human review.
- **Participation-to-change risk:** present. Participation may be sustained without establishing the Topic's separate claim of changed teaching or learner outcomes.
- **Ordinary-group-to-CoP risk:** high. This is the main misuse boundary for the path.
- **Review judgment required:** whether CoP remains supporting or should be absent unless the user's actual study includes sustained shared practice; no genealogy relation follows either way.

### 5.3 `teacher-identity-theory` — `not_recommended`

- **Object actually explained:** professional self-understanding and its contextual interpretation in relation to teaching work.
- **Analysis unit:** teachers' interpreted professional selves, where that is the central object.
- **Materials needed:** for an identity-centred extension, narratives, reflective materials, role expectations, and interactional/contextual accounts that allow interpretation rather than simple trait coding.
- **Insufficient materials:** one attitude item, a demographic category, a programme attendance record, or a self-description treated as complete and stable.
- **Nature of the route:** `not_recommended` is L2 editorial synthesis for this stated question. Suggested identity materials are L3 research guidance. Kelchtermans's professional-self-understanding vocabulary is an L1 candidate pending human review.
- **Participation-to-change risk:** present if an identity account is used to infer learning or changed practice without separate evidence.
- **Ordinary-group-to-CoP risk:** not intrinsic to this path, but combining identity and CoP must not make group membership proof of either a CoP or identity change.
- **Conditional-not-general boundary:** the current route is defensible only as “not recommended as primary for this learning/change question.” It must not be displayed as a general rejection or inferiority claim about Teacher Identity Theory.
- **Review judgment required:** whether “not_recommended/low” is too categorical for the UI and should be approved only with the explicit question-specific qualifier.
