## 2. Evidence semantics

- `source_backed_fact`: source identity/structured fact directly supported by the named record at the listed locator.
- `editorial_synthesis`: Syntag navigation or attribution wording; a nearby source record does not promote it to source fact.
- `research_guidance`: conditional route/risk guidance requiring methods/subject/editorial human review.
- `partially_supported`: the source record supports only a bounded portion or identity, not the full current wording.
- `pending_review`: safe guidance/blank state intentionally awaits methods or human review.
- `blocked`: no reproducible field-level support or no lawful substantive locator; no stronger corpus wording is proposed.
